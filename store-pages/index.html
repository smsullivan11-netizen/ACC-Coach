ACC Coach

The telemetry and coaching companion of a sim-racing team, for Assetto Corsa Competizione.

What it does

ACC Coach is a Windows app that runs in the background (icon next to the clock). It records every session you drive in Assetto Corsa Competizione from the data the game publishes for apps, and uploads your laps to the team's site when the session ends.

On the site, team members compare their laps corner by corner: speed, throttle, brake, steering, gear, racing lines and the time gained or lost everywhere on the lap, with a replay of both cars, leaderboards per track and an Overall Driver Rating. While you drive, the app's live race engineer window follows your fuel, tyre pressures and temperatures, tyre and brake wear and damage, and tells you what to change at the next stop.

Features

  • Records ACC sessions automatically at the game's physics rate
  • Uploads your laps when the session ends
  • Lap comparison with teammates: inputs, racing lines, time gap, replay
  • Leaderboards and Overall Driver Rating
  • Live race engineer: fuel, tyre pressures and temperatures, tyre and brake wear, damage
  • Starts with Windows (can be turned off in Settings > Apps > Startup)

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11 and Assetto Corsa Competizione (Steam)
  • Membership of the team: members sign in to the team site with Discord

Get it

ACC Coach is free and distributed through the Microsoft Store; team members get the link on the team's Discord server.
