ACC COACH

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

ACC Coach (the team site and the ACC Coach desktop app) is a private tool run by a sim-racing team for its own members. It is not a commercial service: there are no ads, no trackers, and no data is sold or shared with anyone outside the team.

What the desktop app records

  • Only while Assetto Corsa Competizione is running, the data the game publishes for apps: inputs (throttle, brake, steering, gear), speed, the car's position on track, lap times and lap validity, and the session details (track, car, session type, the driver name set in the game).
  • For online sessions, from the game's own log file: the name of the server and the balance of performance (ballast, restrictor) the server gave your car. Nothing else is read from the log.
  • Nothing else on your PC: no other programs, files, keystrokes or browsing.
  • Recordings are stored in the app's folder on your PC and deleted 30 days after they have been uploaded. The app also stores the team site's address, your driver name and an upload token that links the PC to your account.

What is uploaded to the team site

  • After each session: the laps (times, validity, lap traces on a 1 m grid, per-corner results).
  • Sign-in uses Discord: your Discord ID, username and avatar are stored. The list of your Discord servers is read only to check that you are a member of the team's server; it is not stored.
  • Setup files you choose to upload to the setups library are stored with your name and visible to every member.
  • Race Day HQ stores race plans, readiness confirmations, team comments and official race updates. During a timed race, the linked desktop app may send the track name and ACC's remaining-session clock every 15 seconds so the team's race clock stays current.
  • Small preferences are kept with your account: which welcome quotes you have already seen and whether you have closed the guide.
  • A sign-in cookie keeps you signed in. No other cookies are used by the site (your browser only remembers whether you prefer corners or sections on lap pages); on the YouTube page, video thumbnails are loaded from YouTube and videos play through YouTube's privacy-enhanced player (no YouTube cookies before you start a video).

Who can see it

Only signed-in team members. Every member can see everyone's laps, comparisons, ratings, driver profiles, race plans and race updates: that is the purpose of the tool.

  • Team news (team records, personal bests, rank promotions, new app versions) is posted in the team's Discord channel.
  • A lap card (an image with the track, the driver's name, the car and the lap time) can be opened by anyone who has its link, so it can be shown on Discord or shared. Members choose to share it; nothing else is public.

Where it is stored, and for how long

  • The site is hosted by Vercel; the database by Neon (Frankfurt, EU).
  • Lap traces are deleted after 60 days, except each driver's best laps and their 10 most recent sessions. Lap times, results and ratings are kept as long as the team uses the site.

Your choices

  • Unlink a PC at any time by revoking its token in Settings; quit or uninstall the app to stop recording.
  • Ask a team admin to delete your data: your account, laps and ratings are removed.

Contact

Questions or requests: contact a team admin on the team's Discord server.
